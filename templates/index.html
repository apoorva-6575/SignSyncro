How SignSyncro Works • User Guide

1. Signing to Speech

  1. Allow camera access when prompted by the browser.
  2. Position your hands inside the guide box on the video preview.
  3. Hold a sign steadily for 0.70 seconds to confirm and add it to the conversation.
  4. Click Speak to read your signed message aloud to hearing partners.

2. Speaking to Sign

  1. Tap Tap to Speak in the voice section.
  2. Speak your sentence naturally.
  3. Review the recognized text in the edit box and click Show Sign & Send.
  4. The SignVisualizer will display the corresponding visual sign sequence.

Troubleshooting

  • Hands not detected? Move hands into the center of the camera frame and increase room lighting.
  • Microphone not responding? Ensure microphone permissions are enabled in your browser settings.
  • Sign not recognized? Check the My Signs tab to confirm the hand shape.
